About ZTA
Curiosity • Creativity • Determination
A Journey Beyond Space

Hello! I'm Rubyat, a 15-year-old adventurer in the world of creativity, technology, and ideas. Ever since I was young, I've been curious about how things work, why they exist, and how I can make them even better.

I love acquiring new skills, specifically in technology and digital creation. Coding, designing, and building projects are my favorite ways to express myself and challenge my imagination.

Other than tech, the outside world interests me too. I enjoy gaming, reading books, discovering new ideas, and learning from creative people around me.

This website is my personal space where I share my journey, projects, experiments, and thoughts with the world. I hope what you find here inspires you to create something amazing too.

I believe age is just a number. Curiosity, creativity, and determination are what truly define a person. Every challenge is another opportunity to grow and improve.

Thanks for visiting my website. Take your time, explore everything, and enjoy the journey with me.

Look up. Explore. Discover.
